# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good faith while requiring evidence, respect privacy, and focus criticism on work rather than people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, or publication of another person's private data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and users. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer profile at https://github.com/rrrrrredy.
