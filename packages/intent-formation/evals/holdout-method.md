# Sealed Holdout Authoring Method

This holdout was authored independently from the explicit schema and intent-class definitions supplied to the author. The author did not access product policy, prior corpora, development tests, generated plugin artifacts, repository history, or earlier sealed-holdout staging material. Neither evaluation arm was run before sealing.

The corpus was constructed to meet the specified class, language, and domain quotas, including the bilingual allocation within every class. Final requirements and unacceptable first-turn behaviors were written directly for each scenario rather than derived from product output. Mechanical validation covered required fields, values, uniqueness, distributions, domain concentration, and internal similarity.

After sealing, the root evaluator will perform mechanical overlap checks against protected prior material without exposing that text to the author. This preserves independent authorship while allowing contamination checks outside the authoring context.
